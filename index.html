Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions our clients ask about our Melbourne unblocking services.

How much does it cost to clear a blocked drain?

Every blockage is different. Simple toilet and pipe clogs can be cleared rapidly at basic plumber rates, while severe tree root invasions require camera localization and water jetting. We provide transparent upfront quotes before starting any work.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, we operate a true 24/7 emergency response line for urgent blockages, flooding toilets, and leaking pipe crises. Our dispatchers will get a licensed local plumber on their way to you immediately.

How long does it take?

Most residential blockages are resolved within 1 to 2 hours once our master plumbers arrive on site and deploy our high-pressure jetters and diagnostic crawlers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. All our technicians are fully licensed master plumbers and Drain Blast carries $20 Million in public liability insurance for your absolute protection and confidence.

Do you service my area?

We cover the entire Greater Melbourne metropolitan footprint—from Western suburbs like Footscray to Eastern suburbs like Hawthorn, Northern hubs, and Southern coastal zones.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX), EFTPOS, and direct bank transfers. We carry convenient mobile payment terminals on all of our service vehicles.

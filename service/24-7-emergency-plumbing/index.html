Signs You Have a Blocked Bathroom Drain

A blocked bathroom drain can start as a slow drain and quickly become a bigger plumbing problem. Look out for these common warning signs.

Slow Draining

Water takes longer than usual to disappear.

Unpleasant Odours

Persistent smells coming from
the drain.

Standing Water

Water pools around your shower or basin.

Overflowing Water

Water backs up when using your shower or basin.

Gurgling Sounds

Unusual sounds coming from your drain.

Recurring Blockages

The drain keeps becoming blocked despite DIY attempts.
